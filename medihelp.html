First Aid Tips

❤️ Chest Pain

Call emergency services immediately for new or severe chest pain. Stop exertion and rest while waiting for help.

🌬️ Breathing Difficulty

Call emergency services for severe breathing difficulty. Help the person sit comfortably and follow the emergency dispatcher's instructions.

🦴 Suspected Fracture

Keep the injured area still and supported. Do not attempt to straighten the bone.

🧠 Head Injury

Seek urgent medical care for a serious head injury. Avoid unnecessary movement of the neck.

⚡ Seizure

Protect the person from nearby hazards. Do not restrain them or put anything in their mouth. Time the seizure. Call emergency services if it lasts 5 minutes or longer.

🌡️ High Fever

Encourage fluids if the person is fully alert and can swallow. Seek urgent help for confusion or breathing difficulty.
